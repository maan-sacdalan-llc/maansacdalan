About

Part strategist, part designer, all in on the mission.

I'm Maan Sacdalan, a Washington, DC based technologist who has spent two decades at the place where design, data and fundraising meet. I started out as a web and graphic designer, grew into front-end development, and went deep on the CRM platforms that power nonprofit fundraising and advocacy.

Over more than 20 years of freelancing for nonprofits, agencies, schools, campaigns and corporations, I've designed and built websites, donation pages, emails and print. Along the way, longtime friends and I founded Arbcentrix to give my clients the dedicated team they deserve while opening doors for talented Filipinos. I also co-founded Stratovation Partners to help organizations plan for what comes next.

I care about work that is well built and well made: donation pages that load fast and convert, data you can trust, emails that render everywhere, and strategy that actually ships.
